## The bet, in two sentences

We believe early-career PMs (months 0-18 in the role) are underserved by current PM-learning content because everything is either generic ("read these 5 books") or aspirational ("here's how a Stripe PM thinks"). Nobody is helping them with the actual case in front of them this week.

PM Companion is an AI-companion product that runs Socratic case studies — gives the user a hypothetical product problem, grades the questions they ask, helps them build PM thinking muscle through repeated practice.

### Founder hypothesis

The PM-learning market is bifurcated:
- **Aspirational content** (Lenny, Reforge): great for senior PMs, reads like inspiration porn for juniors.
- **Interview prep** (Exponent, IGotAnOffer): solves a different job — getting in, not getting better.
- **Bootcamps** (Product School, Pendo): expensive, certification-led, not embedded in daily work.

The gap: a low-stakes, daily, behavioural-practice product for PMs who already have the job. Like Duolingo for product thinking.

### Pricing thoughts

- ₹499/month, ₹4,999/year (~₹417/month equivalent)
- 7-day free trial, no credit card
- Aim for ₹1Cr ARR within 18 months at ~2,000 paid subs

## Open strategic questions

1. **Grading rubric:** how do we score "question quality" objectively enough that users trust it? This is the moat. If grading feels arbitrary, retention dies in week 2.
2. **Content production:** how many cases at launch? 5? 20? 50? Each case is ~3-4 hours to author + calibrate. What's the v1 minimum?
3. **Solo vs. social:** is this a single-player product (Duolingo) or does it need a community layer to drive D7 retention?
4. **Mobile:** web-only for v1 is the plan, but PMs are mobile-first for short-form learning. Are we leaving D7 on the table?
5. **Domain breadth:** consumer apps, B2B SaaS, marketplace, AI products — do we differentiate v1 by domain or stay generic?

## Risks the team is tracking

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation thinking |
|---|---|---|
| Question-grading rubric feels arbitrary | High | Need 2-3 calibrated examples per case before launch |
| Case content quality varies | High | One author per launch, tight QA loop |
| Solo learning weak D7 retention | Medium | Streaks + weekly leaderboard among friends (deferred) |
| Generic ChatGPT eats our lunch | Medium | Differentiate on calibrated grading + structured progression |
| Indian market may not pay ₹499/mo | Medium | 7-day trial, monthly first, founder discount cohort |
