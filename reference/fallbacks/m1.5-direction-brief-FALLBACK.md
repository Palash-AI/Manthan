> **What this is:** Pre-baked sample output for Module 5. Use only if the live module stalls during the workshop.

---

## 1. Reference brief

"Feels like Duolingo's daily-practice loop, for the Anxious Aspirant PM building professional muscle in 15-min reps."

---

## 2. Design vectors (3 picked from lite-03; the rest live in W2)

| Vector | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Mood** (Utility ↔ Cinematic) | Utility, with warmth | Anxious Aspirant needs trust; closer to Notion than to Apple Music |
| **Guidance** (Hidden ↔ Hand-holding) | Hidden until needed | She wants to think, not be told |
| **Copy** (Instructional ↔ Dramatic) | Instructional with friend energy | "Here's a question to try" beats "Embark on your journey" |

---

## 3. Primary emotion

"Sharper. Earned, not flattered."

One adjective + one disclaimer. *Sharper* is the outcome. *Earned, not flattered* is the rejection — we don't want false good-feeling.

---

## 4. AI surface map

| Surface | Job | Input | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Case-study generator | Produces a hypothetical product problem | User's archetype, prior cases | A 200-word case scenario |
| Question grader | Scores user's question 0-10 with one-line "why" | User's question + case context | Score + reasoning |
| Companion responder | Answers user's question, advances the case | Question + case context | Information unlock |
| Report-card synthesiser | Summarises strengths/gaps after the case | Full Q&A transcript | Report card |

Four AI surfaces. Each is its own micro-product with its own prompt, quality bar, and failure modes. Naming them now prevents prompt rot later.

---

## 5. Positioning

"For the Anxious Aspirant PM, PM Companion is a daily practice gym that gives calibrated feedback on her thinking, unlike Lenny's Newsletter which is consumption-only and ChatGPT which doesn't push back."

Names two competitors explicitly. Names what we beat them on. Vague positioning ("the best learning app for PMs") doesn't decide anything; this does.
