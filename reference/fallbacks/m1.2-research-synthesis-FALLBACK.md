## Convergent themes (where 2+ streams agree)

1. **Generic content fails them at the moment of need.**
   - Evidence: User calls 1, 4, 5; Web (Reddit r/ProductManagement).
   - Verbatim: "I read Lenny religiously. When I'm sitting in standup at 9:30 AM, none of it helps." (Riya, Call 1)

2. **They want a feedback signal on their own thinking.**
   - Evidence: User calls 1, 4; Web (multiple Reddit threads).
   - Verbatim: "ChatGPT doesn't grade me." (Karan, Call 4)

3. **Daily, short, habit-shaped beats course-shaped.**
   - Evidence: User calls 3, 5; Company survey.
   - Verbatim: "I want it to feel like practice, not learning." (Aakash, Call 5)

4. **ChatGPT is a competitor — and a weak one.**
   - Evidence: User calls 1, 4; Web (Reddit).
   - Verbatim: "ChatGPT just agrees with whatever I say. The opposite of what I need." (Riya, Call 1)

5. **Solo learning is the preference, not a constraint.**
   - Evidence: User calls 1, 2, 3.
   - Verbatim: "I want practice in low stakes before I make mistakes in high stakes." (Shreya, Call 3)

## Divergent signals (where streams disagree)

1. **Pricing acceptance.**
   - Web (Reddit): "PMs won't pay for learning tools."
   - Company survey: 41% said yes to ₹500/mo.
   - User calls: Karan said "I'd pay ₹500 easily."
   - **Question to investigate:** are the Reddit users a different cohort than our target archetype?

2. **Mobile vs. web.**
   - User calls: 0/5 mentioned mobile as critical.
   - Company doc: assumes web-only is fine for v1.
   - Industry signal: short-form learning is mobile-first.
   - **Question to investigate:** are early-career PMs at-desk daily? Test in v1.

## Anti-patterns to avoid (from competitive scan)

- **Lenny's Newsletter trap:** passive consumption with no behavioural change. We must have a practice loop.
- **Reforge trap:** course-shaped, eight weeks, certificate. We are habit-shaped, daily, no certificate.
- **ChatGPT trap:** agrees with whatever the user says. We must push back and grade honestly.
- **Bootcamp trap:** expensive, time-heavy, theoretical. We are cheap, short, applied.
